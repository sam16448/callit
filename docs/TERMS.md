# Call It: terms and privacy

*Last updated 27 September 2026. Call It is a student project for the RevenueCat Shipaton 2026.*

## What we store

- **Your nickname and avatar**, shown on the leaderboards. No email, no password, no real name.
- **Your runs and answers** (which call you made, your answer, your score), so the server can score fairly and build the boards.
- **An anonymous account id** created by Supabase when you first play. Uninstalling the app or tapping *Start over* leaves that account behind; ask us and we'll delete it.
- **Purchases** are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat. We only learn whether your Call It Pro subscription is active.

We don't sell data, show ads or use trackers.

## Call It Pro

- Monthly ($2.99) or yearly ($14.99) subscription, renewing automatically until you cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
- Pro unlocks unlimited practice, creating leagues, stats, the streak shield and cosmetic effects.
- **Pro never gives points or any advantage on the leaderboards.**

## Fair play

One account per person. Nicknames that are abusive or impersonate someone may be removed. Automated or scripted play may be removed from the boards.

## Questions

Category questions come from the [Open Trivia Database](https://opentdb.com) under CC BY-SA 4.0. Spotted a wrong answer? Report it in the app.
